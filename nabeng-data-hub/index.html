How can we help?

Quick answers about ordering data bundles.

How do I buy a bundle?

Select your network, choose a package, enter the recipient's Ghanaian phone number and continue with the available payment process.

Can I buy data for someone else?

Yes. Enter the recipient's phone number carefully before confirming your order.

How do I get help with an order?

Contact Nabeng Data Hub on WhatsApp at 0599581301 and provide your order reference if you have one.

What if I enter the wrong number?

Check the number before submitting payment. Contact support immediately if you notice an error.
